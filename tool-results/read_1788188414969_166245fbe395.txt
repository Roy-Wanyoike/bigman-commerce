   888→### The killer feature
   889→
   890→I would make the **Super Admin portal an actual platform operating system**, not just another dashboard.
   891→
   892→It should answer:
   893→
   894→> **"What is happening across every lender, every loan, every payment, every workflow and every external provider right now?"**
   895→
   896→And the DCP portal should answer:
   897→
   898→> **"What is happening inside my lending business?"**
   899→
   900→While the customer portal answers:
   901→
   902→> **"What do I owe, what can I borrow, and what do I need to do next?"**
   903→
   904→That separation will make the product much easier to scale to many DCPs while keeping the UX clean.
   905→Yes. I researched this from **Kenyan DCP regulatory requirements, lending/credit operations, collections roles, and established loan-management dashboard patterns**. The biggest correction I would make to the earlier design is:
   906→
   907→> **Do not give every DCP employee the same dashboard with different buttons. Build different workspaces around what each role is accountable for.**
   908→
   909→CBK's DCP framework specifically covers licensing/governance, credit operations, consumer protection, credit information sharing and AML/CFT. It also requires transaction receipts/statements and a complaints-redress mechanism, while placing restrictions on abusive collection practices. ([Central Bank of Kenya][1])

   911→Oracle's lending platform documentation also explicitly separates dashboards for **Loan Officer, Loan Authorizer, Loan Supervisor, Branch Manager and Relationship Manager**, which supports using role-specific workspaces rather than one generic dashboard. ([Oracle Docs][2])
